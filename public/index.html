The Problem

Bridging the Gap Between Waste Generators and Upcyclers

Commodities undergo the linear “take-make-waste” model, leading to ecological deterioration. Recyclable resources — plastics, paper, fabric scraps, cans, and metals — accumulate as unused surplus. Both parties rely on informal methods, bringing about lost opportunities for material recovery. Revora is purpose-built to close this gap.

⟳

Circular Economy

Surplus and scrap materials are diverted from landfills and fed back into production through verified, traceable transactions.

✦

Identity Verified

Every user — individual or entity — is verified via government-issued IDs and TINs using Know-Your-Customer (KYC) regulation before any transaction.

↑

Tiered Memberships

Upcyclers earn their way from Starter to Premium through proven transaction history, ensuring accountability and equitable access for all.

⬡

3PL Integration

Partnered with third-party logistics providers for auto-generated waybills and transparent audit trails that neither party can falsify.

The Process

How Revora Works

Four sequential stages power the complete circular economy lifecycle on Revora.

01

Registration & Verification

Users select their role — Generator or Upcycler — and provide legal credentials (government ID, TIN for entities, branch details). All accounts begin as Pending.

Role Selection Gov’t ID TIN Validation
02

Administrative Approval

Administrators review credentials via a centralized dashboard, governed by a 3–5 business day SLA. Approved accounts are activated; rejected ones are logged with audit history.

SLA Monitoring Audit Logs Queue Priority
03

Tiered Membership

Upcyclers start at Starter tier. After 30 successful transactions, they advance to Regular; 60 more within 6 months earns Premium — with unlimited bulk ordering capacity.

Starter (Max 5) Regular (Max 15) Premium (Unlimited)
04

Verified Transactions

Orders are placed, confirmed, shipped via tracked 3PL logistics, and physically received. “Order Received” triggers the official SLTC & SCTC count update.

KYC Compliance Anti-fraud SLTC/SCTC

Performance-Based Membership

All upcycler accounts begin as Starter upon approval. Advancement is earned through verified, successful transactions — ensuring accountability and equitable access at every level.

UN SDG 12

Responsible Consumption & Production

Revora is intentionally aligned with the United Nations’ 12th Sustainable Development Goal — ensuring sustainable consumption and production patterns. Every feature contributes toward three specific targets:

12.2
Sustainable Management of Natural Resources

Material listings ensure that raw material surplus is displayed and offered, not discarded.

12.5
Substantially Reduce Waste Generation

Upcyclers acquire scraps directly from generators, diverting waste from landfills into active production.

12.6
Encourage Sustainable Practices

Tiered memberships reward entities that actively engage in sustainable material sourcing through transaction milestones.
