The Problem

Bridging the Gap Between Waste Generators and Upcyclers

Commodities undergo the linear “take-make-waste” model, leading to ecological deterioration. Recyclable resources — plastics, paper, fabric scraps, cans, and metals — accumulate as unused surplus. Both parties rely on informal methods, bringing about lost opportunities for material recovery. Revora is purpose-built to close this gap.

⟳

Circular Economy

Revora is built to keep surplus and scrap materials out of landfills and back into production by connecting verified generators and upcyclers.

The Process

How Revora Works

Two implemented stages currently power the registration-and-approval pipeline live on Revora.

01

Registration & Verification

Users select their role — Generator or Upcycler — and provide legal credentials (government ID, TIN for entities, branch details). All accounts begin as Pending.

Role Selection Gov’t ID TIN Validation
02

Administrative Approval

Administrators review credentials via a centralized dashboard, governed by a 3–5 business day SLA. Approved accounts are activated; rejected ones are logged with audit history.
